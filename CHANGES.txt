Zone Intro - changes

1.0.0
- Zone scan: every zone, city, dungeon, raid, battleground, arena, scenario, delve and island expedition on
  the master list (510 places, 499 with lines) was researched from warcraft.wiki.gg. About 4,800 lines in
  total. Places with thin sources keep fewer lines rather than padding; cut or unreleased content has none.
- Places that exist on two continents (Nagrand, Shadowmoon Valley) use the lines for the continent you are on.
- In-game names that differ from the lore page (Hyjal Summit, Blackrock Spire, Dalaran Sewers) are mapped.
- Fixed the settings panel not registering in the AddOns list.
- Every line is labelled in plain text: Lore:, Trivia: or Note:. No colour coding.
- Settings panel (/zoneintro settings): choose which kinds of line appear, plus banner, UI fade,
  first-login and drift options.
- New commands: /zoneintro settings, lore, trivia, notes.

Polish pass
- Smoother pan: one timeline with easing, no snapping while the text drifts.
- Rapid zone changes no longer spam banners (debounce and cooldowns).
- No banner on first login or reload unless "Also show it on first login" is on.
- Opening a panel (character, map, etc.) during the UI fade ends the fade straight away.

Initial version
- Cinematic letterbox banner with zone name and a line of lore on zone change.
